Soundprint

Your listening history,
laid out.

Drop in your Spotify Extended streaming history and get top artists, listening habits, streaks and year-by-year trends.

1 Request your data

Go to spotify.com → Account → Privacy settings and scroll to Download your data. Tick Extended streaming history only, then confirm via email.

2 Wait for the email

Spotify says it can take up to 30 days; it often arrives sooner. Download the zip it links to.

3 Drop it in here

No need to unzip. Parsing happens on your device, in this tab.

🔒 Private by design

This page has no server and no analytics, and a strict content-security policy blocks all network requests once it has loaded. Your files are read in memory and gone when you close the tab. You can verify it yourself: the whole thing is a few hundred lines of plain, readable JavaScript with no dependencies.
